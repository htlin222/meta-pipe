# Eligibility Criteria — Game-based Teaching Strategies in Nursing Education

**Title:** 遊戲導向教學策略於護理教育之成效：系統性回顧與統合分析
**Protocol date:** 2026-05-29

## Inclusion criteria

- **Study design:** Formally published primary research; **randomized controlled trials (RCTs), including cluster-RCTs**.
- **Intervention:** Game-based teaching strategies — gamification, game-based learning (GBL), or serious games — with explicitly described game design mechanics (points, badges, leaderboards, levels, quests, rewards, competition, feedback, unlock mechanics, role/character missions, decision challenges) **or** a complete GBL / serious-game instructional architecture. Applied to nursing-related education or training.
- **Comparator:** Conventional / traditional teaching without game mechanics (e.g., lecture, case discussion, ordinary course/training).
- **Population:** No restriction — nursing students, nursing interns/trainees, clinical nurses, clinical nursing teachers all eligible.
- **Outcomes:** At least one quantitative learning or teaching outcome, with data permitting between-group or pre/post comparison (mean, SD, n, or test statistics).
- **No restriction** on language, publication year, specialty, or participant type.

## Exclusion criteria

- Single-group pre/post studies without a control group.
- Descriptive, cross-sectional, observational, or purely qualitative studies.
- No explicit game design elements / game-based architecture.
- Comparator is also gamified or another competitive intervention (cannot isolate the net effect of game mechanics).
- No concrete quantitative outcomes or no comparable data.

## Outcome classification (NWKM levels)

- **Level 1:** Reaction, engagement, relevance.
- **Level 2:** Knowledge, skills, confidence (learning outcomes).
- **Level 3:** Behavior change, practice application.
- **Level 4:** Patient outcomes / system-level impact.

## Risk of bias / certainty

- RoB 2 (Cochrane) for RCTs; RoB 2 cluster extension for cluster-RCTs.
- GRADE per NWKM outcome level.
